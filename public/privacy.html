Skyway Ops Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Privacy

This notice describes how Skyway Aviation (“Skyway,” “we”) handles information in Skyway Ops, our employee and contractor operations app. It is a product privacy notice, written in plain language. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by an attorney.

1. Who this covers

Skyway Ops is an internal operations console for Skyway Aviation people who fly, dispatch, maintain, or otherwise support Part 135 charter work. It is available at www.skyway.app and as native iOS/Android apps identified as com.flyskyway.ops.

Skyway Ops is not a consumer charter booking marketplace. Customers who book a flight with Skyway Aviation do so through other Skyway channels. This notice applies to people who sign in to Skyway Ops to do their jobs.

2. Account and identity

Access uses Microsoft company single sign-on for @flyskyway.com work accounts. Depending on your role and what you do in the app, we may process:

  • Name, work email, and organization profile from Microsoft
  • Role, approval status, and account state (for example pending, approved, or disabled)
  • Session tokens and related authentication records so you can stay signed in securely

An administrator must approve a profile before it can be used for operational work.

3. Operational data

Work you perform in Skyway Ops is stored so the operation can run. That typically includes records in Firestore and related storage, such as:

  • Trips, schedules, and flight support information
  • Crew assignments, duty and rest records, and related compliance notes
  • Passenger and cargo manifests and check-in records tied to a trip
  • Expenses, receipts, and accounting coordination
  • Maintenance, AOG, and service coordination records
  • Internal messaging and other ops records created in the course of work

This information is used to operate, coordinate, and document Skyway Aviation work. Access is limited by role (for example crew, ops, maintenance, or admin).

4. Location

If you grant permission, Skyway Ops may use when-in-use device location to support operational accuracy — for example confirming where work is being performed, supporting duty or trip context, or locating aircraft/ops activity on maps. We do not need background location for the core product. You can refuse or later revoke location permission in device or browser settings; some operational features may then be less accurate or unavailable.

5. Camera, photos, and files

If you grant permission, you may capture or upload photos and files for work, including:

  • Identification documents used in check-in or trip processing
  • Receipts and expense documentation
  • Maintenance, AOG, or other operational documents

Camera, photo-library, and file access happen only after you allow them. You can upload an existing file instead of using the camera where the product offers that option.

6. Push notifications

On devices where you enable notifications, Skyway Ops may register a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and/or Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token so we can deliver operational alerts. Notification content and deep links may include trip, duty, maintenance, or message context needed to act on the alert. You can disable notifications in device settings.

7. Diagnostics and logging

We collect crash, error, sync, and security logs so we can keep the app working, investigate failures, and protect accounts. Processors used to run Skyway Ops may include:

  • Firebase (authentication, database, storage, messaging, and related services)
  • Vercel (hosting and serverless APIs)
  • Microsoft (company identity and related mail/workspace services)
  • Messaging providers used for in-app ops chat
  • Maps and flight-tracking providers used to display operational location and movement

The exact mix can change as the product changes. We use these services to operate Skyway Ops, not to build advertising profiles.

8. How we share information

We share Skyway Ops information only as needed to run the operation:

  • Service providers / processors who host, transmit, or support the product under our direction
  • Legal, safety, and aviation compliance when we believe we must (for example regulators, insurers, or lawful requests)
  • Other Skyway Aviation personnel whose role requires the record (a dispatcher seeing a trip, maintenance seeing an AOG, and so on)

We do not sell personal data for advertising, and we do not use Skyway Ops data to serve third-party ads.

9. Retention and deletion

Operational records are kept as long as Skyway Aviation needs them for business, safety, and aviation-compliance purposes. Some operational evidence is kept only briefly. For example, passenger identification images captured at check-in are treated as short-lived operational evidence (the product is built around a five-day retention window), not as a permanent ID archive.

Employees and contractors who want an account removed, or who want Skyway Aviation to review deletion of their Skyway Ops data, should contact their company administrator or email ops@flyskyway.com. Account access can also be disabled by an administrator. Deletion or restriction of operational records may be limited where retention is required for safety, legal, or regulatory reasons.

10. Children’s privacy

Skyway Ops is a workplace tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this notice as Skyway Ops changes. The effective date at the top will change when we do. The current version will be posted at https://www.skyway.app/privacy.html.
